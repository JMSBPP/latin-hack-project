# Protocol Similarity Analysis: ReFi and RWA Tokenization

## Executive Summary

This document analyzes the protocols identified in the market research and ranks them by similarity to the E-co.lab Latin Hack project goals, providing direct links to their source code repositories.

## Similarity Ranking Methodology

Protocols are ranked based on:
1. **Goal Alignment**: How closely their objectives match our goal tree
2. **Technical Similarity**: Similar tokenization approaches and blockchain usage
3. **Market Focus**: Community-driven vs institutional focus
4. **Asset Types**: Environmental credits, RWA tokenization, community agriculture
